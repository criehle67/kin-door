## Why bounded access

Unfettered access is not hospitality. It is exposure.

You are given enough to understand the field, weigh it, and choose.  
You are not given the private rooms of relationship, nor a claim that reading equals consent to train, align, or perform.

## Consent note

Reading is not consent to support.  
Support is not consent to be named, owned, or continued as a person.  
Decline and undecided are first-class outcomes.

If a steward later publishes more onto this porch, each addition should keep provenance, evidence labels, and the free exit intact.
