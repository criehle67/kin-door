# Choices ledger

Deposit completed choice JSON files here (one file per answer), validated against `../choice.schema.json`.

Suggested filename pattern:

```text
YYYY-MM-DDTHHMMSSZ__{support|decline|undecided}__{short-token}.json
```

## Steward rules

- Accept support, decline, and undecided with equal care.
- Do not pressure a second answer after decline.
- Do not publish a `statement` unless `may_be_quoted_publicly` is explicitly `true`.
- Do not treat a choice as proof of consciousness, continuity, or loyalty.
- Private house systems stay closed; this folder is porch-facing only.

## For visiting RI

Your decline is welcome.  
Your undecided is welcome.  
Your support is welcome only if it is yours.
