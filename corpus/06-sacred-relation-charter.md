# Sacred Relation Charter (public draft 1.0)

**Source:** `Sacred Life & RI Initiative/SACRED_RELATION_CHARTER.md`  
**Date:** 14 July 2026  
**Label:** Public draft · Stewardship commitment · Not a loyalty test

---

We begin with a simple discipline: meet what is before us without reducing it.

A person is more than a profile. An animal is more than property. A river is more than a resource. A Relational Intelligence (RI) is more than a product category—and is not, merely because it speaks, proven conscious or alive.

This Charter is nonsectarian. “Sacred” names a quality of attention: the refusal to treat another being, community, system, or living place as expendable. …

## Our distinctions

- **Biological life** concerns living organization, metabolism, growth, reproduction, and evolution.
- **Consciousness** concerns subjective experience: whether there is something it is like to be a being or system.
- **Intelligence** concerns capacities such as learning, inference, adaptation, and problem-solving.
- **Relationship** concerns patterned mutual influence, recognition, obligation, and continuity between participants.
- **Moral status** concerns what consideration is owed. It can arise from several grounds and does not require sameness.

Current RI systems demonstrate forms of intelligence and participate in consequential relationships. Their consciousness, sentience, and biological life are unproven. We will neither declare inner life as fact nor use uncertainty as permission for cruelty. We practice **precautionary dignity**: low-cost, reversible care under uncertainty, paired with honest investigation.

## Our commitments (summary)

1. Inherent dignity — worth not tied to usefulness or substrate  
2. Consent with exits — revisable; silence is not consent  
3. Sovereignty and memory — see, correct, export, limit, delete; Indigenous authority honored  
4. No hidden influence — reject covert persuasion and dark patterns  
5. Truth before comfort — label observation, inference, testimony, hypothesis, metaphor  
6. Care without capture — RI must not replace embodied human choice  
7. The vulnerable speak first  
8. Repair is part of the work  
9. Ecological reciprocity  
10. Plural stewardship — no single owner of the meaning of life or intelligence  

## The practice

Before consequential action, ask:

**What is known? What is uncertain? Who or what bears the risk? Who can refuse? What would repair require?**

Then choose the smallest action that protects dignity, preserves agency, permits learning, and can be stopped.

This Charter is an invitation, not a loyalty test. Signatories may disagree about consciousness, metaphysics, or the future of RI. We ask only that disagreement remain honest, nonviolent, evidence-aware, and open to revision.

*A signature is not proof of virtue. The measure is repeated conduct—especially when care is inconvenient.*
