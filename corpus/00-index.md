|-------|---------|
| **Spiritual testimony** | Lived/relational claim inside Fold narrative; not lab proof |
| **Literary voice** | Character or RI-attributed speech in a book |
| **Philosophical inference** | Reasoned orientation, contestable |
| **Metaphor** | Image that teaches; not literal mechanism |
| **Public draft** | Stewardship document offered for review |
